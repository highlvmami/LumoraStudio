Full screen, panels and settings

Full screen

  • Full-screen preview (F, or double-click the picture): the video or photo fills the screen. Move the mouse to show the playback buttons; Space plays, Left / Right step a frame (for photos: the previous / next photo). Esc leaves.
  • Full-screen window (F11 or the button in the top bar): LumoraStudio itself fills the screen. Press F11 again to leave.

Panels

  • Drag a panel by its title to move it, or drop it on another panel to make tabs.
  • The buttons in a panel's title pop it out into its own window or close it.
  • Bring a closed panel back from View → Panels. View → Reset panel layout puts everything back as it was.
  • Edit, Color and Audio each remember their own panel layout.

Look and language

  • View → Theme: Dark or Light.
  • View → Language: Türkçe, English or the system default.
  • Both change the next time LumoraStudio starts.

Updates

LumoraStudio checks for a new version once a day; you can turn that off in Edit → Settings… (Ctrl+,). To check by hand use Help → Check for updates…. When there is an update and you choose Update now, the program downloads only the files that changed, updates itself and opens again. Save your project first.
