Exporting

The Export button at the top right, or Ctrl+E. The What will happen part at the bottom of the window tells you in advance exactly what will be done to the video with the settings you chose.

Quality modes

  • Lossless cut (no re-encoding), recommended: video and sound are copied bit for bit, quality is identical to the source and export takes seconds. Use it when you have only cut and joined. Cuts land on the nearest keyframe; Snap cuts to key frames adjusts the timeline for that.
  • Same as source (re-encode): frame-accurate cuts. Codec, resolution, frame rate, bit depth, colour and bit rate are taken from the source. Used when there are titles, transitions, colour, effects, stickers or freeze frames.
  • Lossless intermediate (FFV1 / MKV): mathematically lossless. Files are very large; for archiving or editing in another program.
  • Custom: you choose codec, size and quality (see below).

Custom settings

  • Preset: YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, ProRes and more.
  • Video codec: H.264, H.265, AV1, ProRes… Your graphics card's encoder is listed as (graphics card) when there is one, and is much faster.
  • Quality mode: Constant quality (recommended), a bit rate, or Target file size (fit into 25 MB, say). Two passes give better quality at the same size.
  • Frame size: including vertical (9:16), portrait (4:5) and square. Fit, black bars or Fit inside, no bars.
  • Audio: Copy when possible, else AAC, FLAC (lossless) or PCM 24-bit.

Subtitles

When the timeline has subtitles you get three choices: Embed as a subtitle track (can be turned on and off in the player), Burn into the picture (always visible, the video is re-encoded) or Save as an .srt file next to the video.

Other exports

  • File → Export current frame… (Ctrl+Alt+S): saves the frame at the playhead as PNG or TIFF.
  • Export subtitles…: only the subtitles, as .srt / .vtt.
