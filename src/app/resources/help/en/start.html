Getting started

LumoraStudio is a free video and photo editor. Its first rule: quality never drops unless you ask for it.

Workspaces

Switch between workspaces with the tabs at the top:

  • Edit: cut and join videos, add titles, stickers and transitions. Editing video
  • Color: correct the colour of the selected clip and check it with scopes. Colour
  • Audio: clean up and level sound, duck music under speech. Sound
  • Photo: crop and edit photos, with layers and masks. Photos
  • How to use: this booklet. F1 opens it at any time.

Your first video, step by step

  1. Choose your videos, sounds and pictures with File → Import media… (Ctrl+I), or drag the files onto the window. They are listed in the Files panel on the left.
  2. Drag a file from Files down to the Timeline, or right-click it and choose Add to timeline.
  3. Play with Space. Split off the parts you don't want with S and remove them with Delete.
  4. When you're done, press Export at the top right. The default Lossless cut mode saves the video without re-encoding, with no quality loss, in seconds.

Projects

Everything you do is kept in a project file (.lumora): File → Save project (Ctrl+S). Your original videos and photos are never changed; the project only remembers what to do with them. When you open a project the media files must still be where they were. Files that cannot be found show as Missing media on the timeline.

Undo

Every change can be undone with Ctrl+Z and redone with Ctrl+Y, or with the arrow buttons in the top bar. The video and photo workspaces keep separate undo histories.

Good to know

  • Rest the mouse on a button or setting to see a short explanation of what it does.
  • Most sliders reset when you double-click them.
  • The smart tools (automatic subtitles, hiding faces, removing backgrounds) run on this computer. The model they need is downloaded once, the first time; your videos are never sent anywhere.
