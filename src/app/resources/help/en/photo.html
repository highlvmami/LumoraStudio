Photos

The Photo tab is a separate editor for photos. The original file never changes: edits are kept in the project and applied when you export.

Opening photos

Open one or more photos (JPEG, PNG, TIFF, WebP and camera RAW files) with Open photos… on the left, or drag them onto the window. Switch between them in the list on the left.

Adjust tab

Light, white balance, colour, colour wheels, curves, HSL and LUT work as for video; see Colour. The histogram at the top shows the spread of tones.

  • Before (\): compare with the original.
  • Reset: removes all edits of the photo.

Cropping

  1. Press Crop (C); the whole photo and a frame appear.
  2. Choose the ratio: Free, Original, 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, 4:5, 9:16.
  3. Drag the frame, then press Crop again. The button next to it removes the crop.

Layers tab

  • Add adjustment layer: colour settings applied to only part of the photo.
  • Mask: where the layer takes effect. Whole picture, Rectangle, Ellipse, Gradient or Brush. Paint on the photo with the brush; hold Alt to erase.
  • Blend and Opacity: how the layer combines with the ones below.
  • Sticker…: put another picture or a video frame on the photo. See Stickers.
  • The top layer is first in the list. Its tick box shows or hides it.

Smart tab

  • Remove background (B): cuts the person or object out in one click and makes the rest transparent. Click again to bring the background back. It is also in the menu you get by right-clicking the photo. Export as PNG, TIFF or WebP to keep the transparency.
  • Improve the result with Model (Fast / Detailed (slow)) and Edge softness.
  • Hide faces and plates: finds and blurs every face and number plate in the photo. Untick the ones that should stay visible.

Exporting

Export photo… (Ctrl+Shift+E). PNG and TIFF are lossless; JPEG and WebP are smaller but lossy. Leaving Bit depth on Automatic keeps the quality; 16-bit avoids banding in smooth gradients. The colour profile is kept and the original file is never overwritten.
