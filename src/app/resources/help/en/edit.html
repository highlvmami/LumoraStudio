Editing video

The Edit workspace has three parts: Files on the left, the viewer in the middle and the Timeline at the bottom. The Properties panel on the right shows the settings of the selected clip.

  • 1 Source / Timeline buttons
  • 2 Playback buttons and timecode
  • 3 Split, join, freeze frame, delete
  • 4 Snapping
  • 5 Position and size

The viewer: Source and Timeline

Two buttons sit above the viewer. Source shows a single file (double-click a file to open it there). Timeline shows the video you are editing.

  1. Double-click a file to open it in Source.
  2. Press I (in) at the start of the part you want and O (out) at its end.
  3. E or the Add to timeline button adds just that range to the end of the timeline.

Double-clicking a picture opens it in the Photo workspace.

Playback

  • Space: play / pause. Left / Right: one frame back / forward. Home / End: start / end.
  • Click or drag in the ruler above the timeline to move the playhead.

Split, delete, join

  • Split at playhead (S or Ctrl+B): cuts the clip under the playhead in two.
  • Delete clip (Delete): removes the selected clip and closes the gap.
  • Join with next clip (Ctrl+J): makes two consecutive parts of the same file one clip again.
  • Drag the start or end edge of a clip to shorten or lengthen it. Drag a clip by its middle to move it.
  • All of these are also on the tool bar above the timeline and in the menu you get by right-clicking a clip.

Freeze frame

Put the playhead on the frame you want and choose Freeze frame… (Shift+F). Enter how many seconds it should stay. That frame holds for this time and then the rest of the clip continues. You can change the length later in Properties.

Tracks

  • The lowest video track is the Main track: your story runs there. The tracks above it (titles, stickers, a second video) are drawn on top of it.
  • Add tracks with Edit → Add video track / Add audio track, or right-click an empty part of the timeline.
  • The buttons in a track's header lock, hide or mute it.

Zoom and snapping

  • + / - or Ctrl + mouse wheel zoom the timeline. Fit shows all of it.
  • With Snapping (N) on, the playhead and clips snap to clip edges.

Moving and resizing the picture

Select a video clip and handles appear around its picture in the viewer. Drag the middle to move it, a corner to resize it, the round handle to turn it. The same settings are under Properties → Position and size (position, Scale, Opacity) as numbers; Reset takes you back.
