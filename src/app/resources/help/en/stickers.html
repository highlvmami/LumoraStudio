Stickers

Put a picture, an animated GIF or another video on top of a video or photo: a logo, an emoji, an arrow, a second camera in a small window…

  • 1 Add sticker…
  • 2 Handles in the viewer: move, resize, turn
  • 3 The sticker clip on the timeline
  • 4 Motion tracking

Stickers on video

  1. Put the playhead where the sticker should appear.
  2. Choose Add sticker… (Shift+K) and pick a picture, GIF or video.
  3. The sticker is placed small on a free track above the main track and is selected.
  4. In the viewer drag it to move it, drag a corner to resize it, drag the round handle to turn it.
  5. Drag its end on the timeline to set how long it stays.

You can also drag a picture from the Files panel straight onto a video track.

Looping stickers

With an animated GIF or a short video selected, turn on Play over and over under Repeat in Properties. The clip plays again and again; drag its end on the timeline to make it as long as you like. Sound is not repeated.

A sticker that follows something

To make a sticker move with a person or object in the picture, see Motion tracking on the Smart video tools page.

Stickers on photos

  1. In the Photo workspace open the Layers tab on the right.
  2. Pick a picture or video with Sticker…. For a video, choose the frame with Video frame (s).
  3. Drag the sticker on the photo, resize it from a corner, turn it with the round handle. Blend it in with Opacity and Blend.
