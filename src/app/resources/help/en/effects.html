Speed and effects

Speed a clip up or put it in slow motion, play it backwards, remove a green screen, pick a ready-made colour filter. It is all in the Properties panel on the right.

The new Properties panel

The panel is sorted into tabs: Edit (text, length, speed, position, transitions), Effects (filters, green screen, picture effects), Smart (stabilise, follow, hide faces, remove the background), Sound and Info. Only the tabs that fit the selected clip are shown. Click a section's title to fold it away; LumoraStudio remembers which ones you keep open. A changed section shows a small dot and a ↺ button to reset it. When the panel is narrow the tabs show just their icons.

Speed, slow motion and reverse

  • Speed: 2 × plays twice as fast, 0.5 × at half speed. The buttons go from 0.25× to 4×. The clip gets shorter or longer on the timeline, and its linked sound changes with it.
  • Play backwards: plays the clip from the end to the start.
  • Speed ramp: gets gradually faster and slower within the clip. Hero slows down for the moment, Bullet is very slow in the middle, Montage goes fast, slow, fast. The small graph shows how the speed changes over the clip.
  • Smooth slow motion: new pictures are drawn between the filmed frames of a slowed clip (optical flow), so it flows without stutter. Mix neighbouring frames is faster. In-between frames with AI gives the cleanest result with fast motion and edges; the preview uses the normal optical flow, the export the AI model (slow).
  • Keep the voice's pitch: faster sound does not sound higher, slower sound not lower.

Clips with a speed change show 2×, ramp or ◀ next to their name on the timeline.

Colour filters

The Colour filters section of the Effects tab has 15 ready-made looks: Vivid, Warm, Cinematic, Golden hour, Black and white… The small pictures show how each looks on your clip. Use Strength to tone it down. Auto colour and light measures the picture and sets exposure, contrast and white balance itself. Filters are also in the Colour workspace and for photos; every setting can still be changed afterwards.

Green screen

  1. Put the clip filmed in front of a green (or blue) screen on a track above the new background.
  2. Turn on Remove the screen colour. If needed, use Pick… and draw a small box over the screen in the viewer to take its exact colour.
  3. Set how much colour goes with Tolerance, the edges with Edge softness, and the green glow on hair with Remove colour spill.

Picture effects

Reduce noise smooths the grain of dark phone videos and keeps edges sharp. Sharpen, Blur, Vignette and Film grain are here too.

Transitions and title templates

The transition list now also has push, circle, clock wipe, doors, flash, pixelate, spin, glitch and zoom blur. With a title selected, click one of the Title templates: your text stays, the look changes (headline, lower third, news band, cinema, neon…). More: Titles, transitions and fades.
