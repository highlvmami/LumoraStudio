Tools

In the Tools menu at the top: smart editing (cutting silences, cutting to the beat, vertical video), screen recording, voice-over, video collage, saving as GIF and processing many photos at once.

Cut silences and hesitations

In talking videos (vlogs, lessons, interviews) this removes the pauses and the "um", "uh", "hmm" in one go. LumoraStudio listens only to the main track; music below it stays where it is.

  • Shortest pause: shorter pauses are a natural part of speaking and stay. 0.6 seconds is a good start.
  • Breathing room: this much of every pause stays before and after the words, so sentences do not run into each other.
  • Remove hesitations: speech recognition runs on this computer; the model is downloaded once, the first time. Choose the spoken language.

It is all one step: if you do not like it, Ctrl+Z brings everything back. The same button is in Properties → Sound.

Cut to the beat

Put the clips on the main track and the music on an audio track below. LumoraStudio finds the beat of the music and moves the cuts between the clips onto it. To use a particular piece of music, select it first; otherwise the longest music clip is used.

  • Move each cut to the nearest beat: your edit stays the same, the cuts just land on the rhythm.
  • Every clip lasts 1, 2, 4 or 8 beats: for a fast montage every clip lasts the same number of beats.

A clip gets only as long as its video allows; clips after the end of the music are left alone.

Vertical video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)

Turns a landscape video into portrait 9:16, 4:5 for Instagram, or square. The picture fills the new frame edge to edge; nothing is stretched. With Keep the speaker in the frame faces are found on this computer and the picture follows the person like a camera operator would: the frame holds still while they move a little, follows smoothly when they walk, and cuts straight to someone else who starts talking.

To move a clip elsewhere, drag it in the viewer. The same button is in Properties → Smart.

Record the screen

Records the screen, with your voice if you like, as a high-quality MP4. After Start recording LumoraStudio steps aside and a small bar shows in a corner; press Stop when done. The recording is saved in the LumoraStudio folder of your Videos folder and added to the Files panel.

Record voice-over

  1. Put the playhead where the narration starts and open Record voice-over….
  2. Choose the microphone; the Level bar should move when you speak.
  3. Press Record. After a three-second count-down the video plays without sound while you talk. Press Stop when done.

The recording is a lossless WAV file and goes on a new sound track exactly where it started. Use headphones so the microphone does not pick up the video's sound.

Video collage

Choose a layout (side by side, four squares, one large and two small…) and tick the videos or pictures that go in it; drag to change their order. Each goes on its own track at the playhead, cropped to fill its cell. Afterwards every clip can still be moved, resized or cropped with Crop under Position and size.

Save as GIF

Makes an animated GIF of the whole video, the selected clips or a part you choose. Size, smoothness and the number of colours decide how big the file gets; the window shows an estimate. GIF has 256 colours; one palette is measured for the whole animation, so the colours do not flicker.

Process many photos

  1. Add photos or a whole folder.
  2. Size: longest side, width, height or a percentage.
  3. Colour: automatic colour and light, and a filter if you like.
  4. Watermark: text or a logo; choose its place, size and opacity.
  5. Save as: file type, quality, folder and name. The name can use {name}, {n} (number) and {date}.

Process photos writes new files; your originals are never changed.
