Colour

In the Color tab you grade the clip selected on the timeline. Scopes are on the left, colour settings on the right, the timeline at the bottom.

Getting started

  1. Select a video clip on the timeline (if none is selected, the clip under the playhead is).
  2. Settings apply to that clip only. Click another clip on the timeline to switch.
  3. Double-click a slider to reset it. Reset colour resets everything.

Settings

  • Light: Exposure, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Whites, Blacks.
  • White balance: Temperature (blue ↔ yellow) and Tint (green ↔ magenta).
  • Colour: Saturation and Vibrance. Vibrance hardly touches skin tones.
  • Colour wheels: Lift changes the shadows, Gamma the midtones, Gain the bright areas. Drag a wheel towards a colour; hold Shift for fine control.
  • Curves: click and drag to add points; double-click or right-click a point to remove it. Work on RGB or a single channel.
  • HSL mixer: change the hue, saturation and lightness of one colour range (only the blue of the sky, say).

LUT

Load a .cube colour table with LUT → Browse… and set its strength with Amount (%). Write a look you like to a .cube file with Save look as LUT… to use it on other clips, photos or in other programs.

Scopes

Your eyes can be fooled, scopes cannot. The Waveform shows brightness, RGB parade each colour channel separately, the Vectorscope hue and saturation, the Histogram the spread of tones. A white wall, for example, should reach the same height in all three channels of the RGB parade.

Graded clips are re-encoded on export. See Exporting.
