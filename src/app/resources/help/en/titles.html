Adding a title

  1. Put the playhead where the title should appear.
  2. Choose Add title (T). The title goes on a track above the main track. You can also right-click a track and choose Add title here.
  3. With the title selected, type the text in Properties and set the Font, size, Text colour, Alignment and Position.
  4. For readability add an Outline, a Background box or a Soft shadow.
  5. Choose how it comes and goes with the In animation and Out animation: Fade, Slide up, Pop, Typewriter.
  6. Change the title's length by dragging its edge on the timeline.

Adding a transition

Transitions go between two neighbouring clips on the main track.

  1. Select the clip on the right, or move the playhead near the cut.
  2. Add transition (Ctrl+T) adds a cross dissolve.
  3. Under Properties → Transition from the previous clip change its type (dissolve, dip to black / white, wipes, pushes, zoom, blur) and length.

A transition needs some extra picture on both sides of the cut. If a clip ends exactly at the end of its file, trim a few frames first. To remove a transition, right-click the clip and choose Remove transition.

Fade in and fade out

Select a clip and enter the Fade in and Fade out lengths under Properties → Fades. The sound rises and falls with the picture.
