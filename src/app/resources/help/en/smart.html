Smart video tools

These tools appear in the Properties panel when you select a clip on the timeline. They all run on this computer; the model they need is downloaded once, the first time.

  • 1 Stabilisation
  • 2 Hide faces and plates
  • 3 Background

Stabilising shaky footage

  1. Select a hand-held, shaky clip.
  2. Under Stabilisation turn on Steady the shaky camera. The camera movement is measured once.
  3. Smoothing: how much of the movement is removed. High values also remove slow sway.
  4. The zoom setting enlarges the picture a little so the moving edges stay outside the frame.

Motion tracking

  • 1 Motion tracking: shown when a clip on a track above the main track is selected

Makes a title, sticker or blur move with something in the video below it (for example a name above a walking person).

  1. Select a clip on a track above the main track (title, sticker…).
  2. Under Motion tracking press Follow an object….
  3. Draw a box around the object to follow in the viewer (Esc cancels).
  4. When tracking is done, play the clip to check it. Remove tracking undoes it.

Hiding faces and number plates

  1. Select a video clip and under Hide faces and plates press Find faces and plates.
  2. LumoraStudio finds every face and number plate and follows them as they move.
  3. Look: Blur, Pixelate or Black box. Strength sets how strongly they are hidden.
  4. Untick the ones that should stay visible (your own face, for example).
  5. If you lengthened the clip afterwards, Search again covers the new part too.

Removing the background

  1. Select a video clip and turn on Remove the background. The person or object in front stays, the rest becomes transparent.
  2. Model: Fast or Detailed (slow). The detailed model finds hair and fine edges better.
  3. Soften the cut-out with Edge softness.

On the main track the background turns black. To show another picture behind, put the clip on a track above that picture. Playback slows down while background removal is on.
